# Discovery record and acceptance boundary

15 September 2026.

The current continuation began by rereading the named Split-Zero sources, then computing the original cell/bone incidence matrices on release supports. Exact rational nullspaces exposed the distinguished class at successive prefixes: for d=4 the fifth class is nonzero through prefix 14 and is an integer boundary at prefix 15. A local exact solve produced the printed ten-term signed preimage on 24 original cells. The acceptance is its explicit integer boundary identity and unbounded support-membership check, not the linear solver's return.

Exploratory nonnegative optimization on maximal omissions produced candidate dual cochains. Their integer coefficients, all original generator evaluations, and unbounded-parameter affine support tests are independently replayed by verify.py without an optimizer. Solver infeasibility statuses are not part of the proof. The final duals classify the original entire release dictionary, not just a solver-selected shortlist of new tiles.

Additional constant-charge annulus searches imposed 120-degree rotational symmetry on the original bone orbits. They returned positive covers for (d,h)=(3,0),(3,1),(4,0),(4,1),(5,0),(5,1),(6,0), and no solution statuses for (3,2),(3,3),(4,2) under that symmetry restriction. These were exploratory data only; no impossibility claim for unrestricted annuli or general h is made. Those searches did not enter the present proof. Exact rational image checks on several larger annuli are also exploratory and are not a positive-cover assertion.

Finite tests of the Split-Zero implementation are executed tests; the all-object universal property and all-parameter mathematics are in PROOF.md. No new Lean run or external specialist acceptance has been performed.
