Use it to select a cataloged work or propose another verifiably open work, choose the exact target language and written standard, and complete source, rights, component, and baseline-build preflight. A source-bound job such as the [Open Logic packet](openlogic-v1.md) can be run directly after its recorded download identity is verified.

The workflow is model- and vendor-neutral. It requires separate monolingual source and target editions, cumulative editable returns, exact manifests and checkpoints, and independent language and mathematical review. A build replay or model agreement is not linguistic or mathematical certification.
