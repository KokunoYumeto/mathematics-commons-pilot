# First 30-day pilot launch board

**Status:** pre-launch; recruitment and record selection are open, but no live Research Packet queue exists

**Date:** 7 August 2026

**Purpose:** turn the operational MVP into one small, auditable multi-contributor cycle

The first pilot is a workflow test, not a theorem race. Its success conditions are durable evidence, correct provenance, useful negative results, detected errors, independently reviewed bounded outputs, and a candid retrospective. Solving an open problem is neither required nor enough to bypass the review gates.

## What exists now

- a public, DOI-bearing concept repository and protected integration path;
- a Leiden-led governance, attribution, responsibility, disclosure, and rights framework;
- versioned problem, packet, transition, source, run, evidence, and review contracts;
- local structural, semantic, adversarial, and handoff checks;
- an elementary no-novelty mechanics calibration; and
- a [source-audited candidate docket](PILOT_CANDIDATES.md) with seven established-problem records and two provisionally shortlisted community-originated status-reconciliation records.

The repository also includes a [one-way mirror runbook](MIRROR_RUNBOOK.md). A second independently controlled destination is still a launch gate, not a completed feature.

The live queue, named roster, reserved review capacity, and real packet handoffs do not yet exist. Passing software checks establishes process conformance only; it does not establish mathematical truth, novelty, reviewer independence in fact, or a lawful basis that has not been checked by a person.

## Contributions needed for launch

| Lane | Bounded first contribution | Independence requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot coordination | Keep the 30-day board, capacity limits, and retrospective current. | May not turn schedule pressure into claim acceptance. |
| Record stewardship | Freeze one statement, source/status assessment, provenance trail, and first packet. | Another person checks material source/status claims. |
| Literature review | Open exact sources and reconcile what they establish. | Do not infer absence or novelty from an unsuccessful search. |
| Mathematical skepticism | Reconstruct or attack one exact argument and its edge cases. | Must not be the producer approving the same facet. |
| Computation review | Replay one bounded computation and inspect completeness/certificates. | Independent implementation or clean-environment replay where feasible. |
| Formalization | Compile or build one scoped formal artifact and report the trust base. | Separate correspondence review checks the intended statement. |
| Repository and security | Test schemas, validators, handoff boundaries, CI, and recovery. | Report bypasses; do not weaken gates to make a run pass. |
| Rights and provenance | Check source identity, permitted uses, attribution, and bundle boundaries. | Unresolved third-party material stays metadata-only or private. |
| Exposition and access | Make one accepted artifact clearer or usable across a practical access barrier. | Mathematical meaning and source attribution receive their own review. |

[Volunteer through the structured public form](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/mathematics-commons-pilot/issues/new?template=pilot_volunteer.yml). A stable public pseudonym is sufficient. Role assignment is based on the declared contribution and review scope, not credentials, biography, model tier, or compute ownership.

## Candidate selection

The [candidate docket](PILOT_CANDIDATES.md) proposes:

- three first-wave records testing formal replay, status reconciliation, and statement repair;
- two second-wave records testing solved-result provenance reconciliation and certificate-backed finite computation;
- two visible but deliberately narrow established-problem reserve anchors; and
- two community-originated resolved/status-reconciliation records whose exact statements, rights layers, and prior-art positions still require packet-level review.

The recommended capacity boundary is seven active records with Erdős #242 and #993 held in reserve unless reviewer and computational capacity are explicitly available. “Source-audited docket” means the shortlist's public claims and links received a dated preflight; it does not replace the deeper source/status review required for a live `problem_record`.

A prose listing is not a live record. Before selection, a record must have an exact validated problem snapshot, dated qualified status assessment, source and rights record, responsible steward, bounded useful packet, resource limits, and reserved independent-review lane. The stewards publish the reasons for selection, deferral, or rejection.

## Proposed cycle boundary

Once the launch gates pass, Day 1 freezes the roster, record set, first packet snapshots, reviewer reservations, and base commit. Day 30 freezes the exact record graph, accepted and rejected evidence, challenges and corrections, reviewer-load report, clean-clone reconstruction receipt, and retrospective. Unfinished work is carried forward explicitly; it is never promoted to make the calendar look successful.

Until that Day 1 freeze exists, the honest invitation is: inspect the concept and code, challenge a specific weakness, source-audit a candidate, nominate a precise community conjecture, or declare a bounded contribution/review lane.
