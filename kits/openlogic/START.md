# Start here

Before translating, verify `MANIFEST.sha256`, then read `JOB.json`, `SOURCE.json`, `BUILD.json`, `NOTICE.md`, `PROMPT.md`, `QA.md`, and `RETURN.md`.

Ask the user:

1. What exact target language, locale, script, and orthographic standard should this edition use?
2. Who are the intended learners, and what mathematical register should the edition use?
3. What existing editions or overlapping work are known, and is this an independent edition or a contribution to one of them?
4. Who can review the language and the mathematics independently?

Do not translate before those answers are recorded in `SOURCE.json`. Unknown edition coverage is not evidence that no edition exists.
