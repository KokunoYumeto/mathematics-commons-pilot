# Independent review

A fresh reviewer must check the frozen candidate against the exact source and report findings without editing it during the review.

The review covers every completed unit, formula, theorem and proof boundary, exercise, answer, cross-reference, citation, figure, stable identifier, terminology decision, omission ledger, component notice, and build result. It also checks that the reader is monolingual and that `source/` remains byte-identical.

`QA.json` remains `not_run` until this review occurs. Compilation is not linguistic or mathematical certification. A failing review returns exact defects and the last good cumulative checkpoint; production repairs them in a new generation and then requests another cold review.
